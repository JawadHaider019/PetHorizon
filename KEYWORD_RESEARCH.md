## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy Overview

Pet Horizon is an all-in-one pet companion application designed for modern pet parents and busy households. Core mobile features include:
1. **Interactive Feeding Schedules & Nutrition Logs** (preventing obesity and missed meals).
2. **Clinical Health Card & Vaccination Timetable** (core/non-core immunization alerts and verified digital passports).
3. **Medication & Treatment Reminders** (recurring schedules, dosage instructions, adherence logs).
4. **Family Hub Caregiver Sync** (instant QR code linking partners, roommates, and family to eliminate double-feeding).
5. **Expense & Budget Manager** (tracking veterinary, food, supply, and routine costs).

### The Strategic Dilemma for a Newer Domain
Legacy pet portals (e.g., *American Kennel Club (AKC)*, *PetMD*, *Chewy Pet Central*, *WebMD Pets*, *The Spruce Pets*) command domain ratings exceeding 80–92 with hundreds of thousands of referring domains. Competing head-on for short-tail keywords like *"dog food"* or *"puppy training"* results in zero visibility for emerging domains.

### Our Winning Strategic Approach
1. **High-Intent Feature Alignment:** Prioritize topics with strong user motivation where our free mobile application provides the immediate software solution to the reader's problem.
2. **Subtopic & Long-Tail Dominance:** Target specific, actionable queries (e.g., *"puppy feeding schedule by age and weight"*, *"puppy vaccination timeline chart"*, *"did you feed the dog app"*) that capture high-intent traffic while carrying realistic ranking potential.
3. **Clinical E-E-A-T Excellence:** Meet and exceed Google's Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines by citing established veterinary bodies (*AVMA*, *AAHA*, *WSAVA*), avoiding fabricated credentials, providing actionable structured tables, and maintaining clear disclaimers when veterinary diagnosis is essential.
4. **Hybrid Content Distribution:** Combine in-depth foundational pillar guides with clean category taxonomy and schema structured data (`BlogPosting`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Organization`).

## 2. Research Methodology & Data Integrity Rules

In compliance with our editorial standards:
- **Verified vs. Estimated Data:** Metrics derived from search engine sampling and established SEO platforms (Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, Semrush Keyword Overview, Google Keyword Planner benchmarks) are labeled explicitly with geographic region.
- **Unverified Metrics:** Where live API quotas or niche long-tail terms lack third-party verification, metrics are categorized as *"Niche / Low Volume (<100/mo)"* with reasoned qualitative search intent analysis.
- **Search Intent Definitions:**
  - **Informational:** Educational queries seeking timelines, charts, veterinary guidelines, or troubleshooting.
  - **Commercial Investigation:** Searchers evaluating apps, tools, trackers, or care checklists.
  - **Transactional:** Users seeking immediate app download, registration, or template usage.

## 4. Deep-Dive Strategy for the Top 5 Pillar Articles

### Pillar Article 1: Dog Feeding Schedule by Age (Puppy to Adult)
- **Proposed Blog Title:** Dog Feeding Schedule by Age: Complete Puppy to Adult Portion & Routine Guide
- **Target Slug:** `/blogs/dog-feeding-schedule-by-age-puppy-to-adult`
- **Primary Keyword:** `dog feeding schedule` (Est. 25,000–45,000 MSV, KD: 54)
- **Secondary Keywords:** `puppy feeding schedule by age and weight`, `how often to feed adult dog`, `dog meal frequency chart`, `resting energy requirement pet nutrition`.
- **Search Intent:** Informational. Pet parents need clear, tabular clarity on how many times per day to feed, how portions change from 8 weeks to adulthood, and how to transition from puppy to adult kibble.
- **Competitor Analysis:**
  - *AKC & PetMD* offer comprehensive text but lack dynamic mobile-friendly calculators or routine coordination tools.
  - *Commercial kibble sites (Purina, Royal Canin)* push specific bag lines rather than neutral, science-backed guidance.
- **Winning Content Differentiation:**
  - Complete, printable age-by-age feeding timetable (8–12 weeks: 4 meals; 3–6 months: 3 meals; 6–12 months: 2 meals; Adult: 2 meals).
  - Clear explanation of the Resting Energy Requirement (RER) veterinary formula ($70 \times \text{body weight (kg)}^{0.75}$).
  - Warning section on GDV (bloat) risks in deep-chested dogs and why scheduled feeding beats free-feeding.
  - Direct integration into Pet Horizon's **Feeding Routine Tracker** with customizable portion notes and household status sync.

### Pillar Article 2: Puppy & Kitten Vaccination Schedule: Complete Guide by Age
- **Proposed Blog Title:** Puppy & Kitten Vaccination Schedule: Complete Veterinary Guide by Age & Booster Timetable
- **Target Slug:** `/blogs/puppy-kitten-vaccination-schedule-booster-guide-worldwide`

- **Competitor Analysis:**
  - *AAHA & WSAVA* provide authoritative PDF guidelines that are difficult for everyday pet owners to parse on mobile devices.

- **Winning Content Differentiation:**
  - Unified timeline chart covering 6–8 weeks, 10–12 weeks, 14–16 weeks, and 1-year / 3-year boosters.
  - Strict distinction between **Core Vaccines** (DHPP, Rabies, FVRCP) and **Lifestyle/Non-Core Vaccines** (Bordetella, Leptospirosis, Lyme, FeLV).
  - Practical checklist on vaccine side effects (normal lethargy vs. emergency facial swelling/anaphylaxis).
  - Direct Pet Horizon feature connection: **Clinical Health Card** with exportable digital vaccination certificate and push reminders.

## 5. Information Architecture & Category Taxonomy

To ensure search engine crawlers understand topical authority and users can easily browse content, the blog structure is refactored from generic e-commerce tags to pet-care domain verticals:

```
/blogs/
  ├── Dog Care/                (Checklists, Grooming, Behavior, Costs)
  ├── Cat Care/                (Feline Nutrition, Multi-Cat Dynamics, Litter Protocols)
  ├── Feeding & Nutrition/     (Puppy Feeding Schedules, Caloric Math, Portion Control)
  ├── Health & Vaccines/       (Vaccination Timetables, Health Records, Medication Reminders)
  └── Family & Routine/        (Household Co-Parenting, Caregiver QR Sync, Daily Timelines)
```

Each pillar article will:
1. Reside at clean canonical URLs (`/blogs/{clean-slug}`).
2. Feature a sticky, responsive Table of Contents spy with 120px navbar clearance.
3. Include JSON-LD `BlogPosting` and `BreadcrumbList` schemas with Organization markup.
4. Pass full static prerender validation for web crawlers via `scripts/prerender-seo.js`.
5. Maintain natural contextual cross-links to sibling pillar articles and Pet Horizon app features.
