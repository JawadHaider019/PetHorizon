## 1. E-E-A-T & YMYL Editorial Philosophy

Pet care, animal nutrition, medication dosing, and biosecurity regulations fall directly under Google's **Your Money or Your Life (YMYL)** quality guidelines. Erroneous advice can lead to severe animal harm, toxicity, or legal penalties.

### The Pet Horizon Transparency Standard:
1. **Editorial Team Attribution:** All guides are published under the verified collective byline **"Pet Horizon Editorial Team"** or **"Pet Horizon Care Team"**.
2. **Zero Persona Fabrication:** In strict adherence to our ethical guidelines, Pet Horizon **never invents fictitious veterinarians, fabricated medical credentials, or bogus clinical advisory boards**.
3. **Consensus Veterinary Sources:** All clinical metrics (Resting Energy Requirements, core/non-core vaccination schedules, medication intervals, and periodontal grading) are explicitly grounded in consensus guidelines published by recognized institutions:
   - **AAHA** (American Animal Hospital Association)
   - **WSAVA** (World Small Animal Veterinary Association)
   - **AVMA** (American Veterinary Medical Association)
   - **APOP** (Association for Pet Obesity Prevention)
   - **Cornell Feline Health Center**
   - **AHS** (American Heartworm Society)
   - **Defra / BVA** (UK Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs / British Veterinary Association)
   - **DAFF / AVA** (Australian Department of Agriculture / Australian Veterinary Association)
   - **CFIA / CVMA** (Canadian Food Inspection Agency / Canadian Veterinary Medical Association)
4. **Mandatory Educational Disclaimers:** Every clinical and pharmaceutical guide contains explicit language clarifying that content is for educational purposes only and cannot substitute for individualized veterinary examination and diagnostic bloodwork.
