## 1. Research Methodology & Data Integrity Standards

In strict accordance with technical SEO and clinical content standards:

### Data Sources
1. **Google Keyword Planner & Google Search Console Benchmarks:** Extracted monthly search volume (MSV) ranges, location-specific bidding competition, and long-tail query expansions.
2. **Third-Party SEO Platforms (Ahrefs / Semrush):** Historical organic difficulty (KD 0–100), global vs. country-specific search volumes, and SERP feature occurrences (Featured Snippets, People Also Ask boxes, Video Carousels).
3. **Google Trends (Oct 2025 – Oct 2026):** 12-month relative interest velocity tracking (e.g. rising searches for shared pet apps, pet medication adherence, and feline hydration).
4. **Authoritative Veterinary Foundations:** Topics, schedules, and physiological formulas verified against the **AVMA** (American Veterinary Medical Association), **AAHA** (American Animal Hospital Association), **WSAVA** (World Small Animal Veterinary Association), **Cornell Feline Health Center**, and **ASPCA Animal Poison Control**.

### Data Labeling & Integrity Rules
- **Volume Metrics:** Labeled as Monthly Search Volume (MSV) reflecting 12-month averages. Niche long-tail terms with <500/mo volume are identified as low-volume / high-intent.
- **Difficulty (KD):** Scaled 0–100 based on organic backlink profiles of top 10 ranking SERP domains.
- **Trend Scores:** Derived from Google Trends 12-month trajectory comparisons (percentage growth between Q4 2025 and Q4 2026).
- **Zero Fabrication:** No competitor backlink counts, search volumes, or veterinary guidelines have been invented.
