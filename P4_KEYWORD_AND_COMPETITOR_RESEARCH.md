## 3. Keyword Cannibalization Audit & Resolution Strategy

To protect Pet Horizon's search authority, we audited potential query conflicts between informational articles and interactive calculators:

### 1. Calorie Guide vs. Calorie Calculator
- **Competing URLs:**
  - `/blogs/pet-calorie-calculator-feeding-guide-apop` (Informational Guide)
  - `/tools/pet-calorie-calculator` (Interactive Web Application)
- **Cannibalization Analysis:**
  - Intent Differentiation: The blog post targets users searching for the *mathematical explanation* of RER/MER and metabolic formulas, while the tool targets users who want *instant calculations*.
  - Title & H1 Alignment: Blog H1 is `"Pet Calorie Calculator: How to Calculate Resting Energy Requirements (RER)"`; Tool H1 is `"Pet Calorie & Daily Portion Calculator (Dogs & Cats)"`.
  - Canonical & Cross-Linking: Both declare distinct canonical URLs. The blog article links directly into the interactive tool as its featured interactive hero component. No consolidation required.

### 2. Worldwide vs. Regional City Guides
- **Competing URLs:**
  - `/blogs/puppy-kitten-vaccination-schedule-booster-guide-worldwide` vs. `...-new-york`
  - `/blogs/household-pet-coparenting-feeding-routine-sync-worldwide` vs. `...-london`
  - `/blogs/monthly-pet-care-cost-vet-expense-budget-worldwide` vs. `...-toronto`
  - `/blogs/best-pet-gps-trackers-health-wearables-review-worldwide` vs. `...-sydney`
- **Cannibalization Analysis:**
  - Intent Differentiation: Worldwide guides cover broad consensus standards (AVMA, WSAVA, ISO), while city pages address legally binding local regulations (NYC DOHMH health code, UK microchipping fines, Toronto licensing, Sydney bushwalk paralysis ticks).
  - Cross-Linking Architecture: Each worldwide guide acts as a cluster pillar linking to its regional supporting page, preventing internal dilution.
