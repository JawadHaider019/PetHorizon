# Pet Horizon — 90-Day Organic Search Growth Roadmap

A sustainable, evidence-based SEO strategy targeting English-speaking pet owners in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia.

---

## Month 1: Foundation, Indexation & Search Console Calibration (Days 1–30)

### Week 1–2: Indexation & Google Search Console Verification
- **Google Search Console (GSC) Setup:**
  - Submit `https://pethorizon.jawumitech.com/sitemap.xml`.
  - Request inspection and indexing for the homepage, `/blogs` hub, and both interactive calculator tools (`/pet-calorie-calculator`, `/pet-age-calculator`).
  - Monitor the *Coverage / Pages* report for genuine 200 OK responses and verify zero 404/soft-404 false alarms.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools & IndexNow Ping:**
  - Connect Bing Webmaster Tools using existing DNS or meta verification.
  - Submit IndexNow key (`c987a26f34e142b9a76d8b9e5c1204d8.txt`) for instant notification of updated routes.
- **Schema Validation in Rich Results Test:**
  - Test top 5 blog articles in Google's Rich Results Test tool to verify valid `BlogPosting`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `Organization` markup.

### Week 3–4: Baseline Rank Tracking & Search Query Discovery
- **Rank Tracking Setup:**
  - Track core commercial queries: *"best pet care app"*, *"pet medication reminder app"*, *"shared pet care routine"*, *"free dog health tracker"*.
  - Track high-volume informational queries: *"puppy vaccination schedule"*, *"dog feeding schedule by age"*, *"feline kidney disease hydration"*, *"puppy potty training schedule"*.
  - Track programmatic local queries: *"puppy vaccination schedule new york"*, *"pet routine sync london"*, *"monthly pet care cost toronto"*, *"best pet gps trackers sydney"*.
- **Search Intent Gap Analysis:**
  - Review GSC *Performance* data after 30 days to discover high-impression, low-CTR queries (positions 8–20) for targeted meta description and H2 refinement.

## Month 2: Topical Authority, Content Velocity & Tool Syndication (Days 31–60)

### Week 5–6: Expand Veterinary Topical Clusters
- **New Cluster Publishing (2 articles/week):**
  - **Canine Nutrition & Weight Management:** Articles targeting *"low calorie dog treats for training"*, *"dog body condition score (BCS) chart"*.
  - **Feline Wellness & Senior Care:** Articles targeting *"cat litter tracking app"*, *"early signs of arthritis in older cats"*.
- **Internal Linking Reinforcement:**
  - Cross-link newly published articles into existing flagship guides (#1 *Dog Feeding Schedule*, #12 *Feline Kidney Disease*, #17 *Pet Calorie Calculator*).

### Week 7–8: Interactive Tool Outreach & Embeddable Widgets
- **Calculator Outreach Campaign:**
  - Package the Pet Calorie Calculator and Pet Age Calculator as free resources for pet rescue shelters, adoption organizations, and foster networks in the US, UK, CA, and AU.
  - Offer embeddable iframe / badge versions of the calculator tools to ethical pet adoption blogs in exchange for contextual editorial backlinks.

## Month 3: Off-Page Authority, E-E-A-T Amplification & International Scaling (Days 61–90)

### Week 9–10: Digital PR & Ethical Backlink Acquisition
- **Help A Reporter Out (HARO) / Connectively & Featured.com:**
  - Respond to journalist queries regarding household pet co-parenting trends, pet healthcare expenses, and puppy routine training.
  - Position the Pet Horizon Care Team as institutional contributors on pet technology and preventive care routines.
- **Shelter & Humane Society Resource Pages:**
  - Reach out to regional shelter resource pages that list recommended new-puppy checklists (#5) and the 3-3-3 rescue dog decompression guide (#37).

### Week 11–12: Quarterly Audit, Optimization & Conversion Lift
- **Content Refresh Protocol:**
  - Update any articles showing keyword rank stagnation with updated clinical studies, refreshed charts, or additional FAQ subsections.
- **CRO & Funnel Analysis:**
  - Analyze organic traffic conversion rates to Google Play Store downloads and iOS waitlist registrations.
  - Optimize blog conversion cards and sticky header download buttons to maximize mobile visitor conversion.
