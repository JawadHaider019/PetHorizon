## 2. On-Page SEO & Content Quality Enhancements

### 2.1 E-E-A-T and Search Quality Rater Alignment
- **Removal of Fictitious DVM Personas:** Earlier versions referenced fictitious doctor names. In Google's high-stakes YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) landscape, falsified credentials invite algorithmic penalties. All persona bylines were unified under the institutional **Pet Horizon Editorial Team** and **Pet Horizon Care Team**.
- **Clinical Citations Component (`BlogCitationCard`):** Embedded authentic, clickable reference badges directly beneath article titles, attributing clinical protocols directly to the world's leading veterinary organizations.

### 2.2 Intent Matching & Micro-Conversion CTAs
- **Informational Guides:** Articles like *Dog Feeding Schedule* and *Feline Kidney Disease* guide the reader with practical timelines and calorie formulas, offering seamless contextual conversion points to Pet Horizon's digital feeding reminders and hydration tracking.
- **Commercial & Software Review Guides:** Articles like *Best Pet Care Apps for 2026* and *Pet GPS Trackers* provide unbiased comparative matrices, positioning Pet Horizon as the free, ad-free household co-parenting alternative.
- **Local Geotargeted Guides:** Geotargeted articles (New York, London, Toronto, Sydney) incorporate country-specific regulatory protocols (e.g., USDA in the US, DEFRA in the UK, OVMA in Canada, and AVA in Australia).
